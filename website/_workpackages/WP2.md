leader: Krishnendu Chatterjee

For some classes of probabilistic systems termination is a (semi-)decidable property. This includes 
finite-state Markov chains, specialized probabilistic pushdown automata,
one-counter probabilistic programs and constant probability loops. These decidable fragments are,
however, limited in their expresivity.
When allowing more expressive program models, termination becomes harder, and it can become possible to encode the Skolem-Problem in the program. 

We will study the decidability/hardness of termination of more expressive probabilistic programs, in particular also focusing on certificates for those programs.
