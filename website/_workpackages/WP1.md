number: 1
title: Controlling Termination
leader: Ezio Bartocci
---


Termination of probabilistic loops can be certified through proof rules that require finding expressions that satisfy certain conditions.

Synthesizing such expressions is undecidable in general and
current approaches can fail even for simple programs. We will develop "relaxed" proof rules for termination for certain classes
of programs, and develop new synthesis techniques.
